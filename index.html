About Our School

Tido Comprehensive School is a public educational institution in Kolwa Central, Winam, Kenya. Sponsored by a religious organisation, the school offers KCPE, KPSEA, and KJSEA certificates under the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC) and Ministry of Education.

The school has 1,250 pupils and 30 teachers. Classes of approximately 60 learners are divided into two streams — Champions and Achievers — reflecting values of independence, integrity, and courage.

Facilities include a library, laboratory, debate halls, kitchen, computer labs, and sports facilities. Teachers nurture pupils across ICT, sciences, sports, music, and economics.
